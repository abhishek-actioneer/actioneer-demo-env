Clarity issues to resolve
Concern 01

Column height with 4 satellites

Hub + commentary + follow-ups + challenge = very tall clusters. After Challenge deck runs, vertical scroll within horizontal scroll becomes awkward. Commentary and follow-ups could default collapsed.

Concern 02

Two chat entry points, one action

Clicking commentary and clicking "Ask about this" both open a chat panel but load different things. The visual affordance needs to distinguish "open existing report" (commentary ↗) from "start new thread" (Ask about this →).

Resolved in mockup

"Refresh all" label underscopes the action

Since refresh now triggers deep research per chart, Refresh all is misleading. Renamed to ↻ Re-analyze to signal the full pipeline runs — not just a data pull.

Resolved in mockup

Follow-ups look like static text

Follow-up questions need a hover affordance — arrow animates right on hover. Each item is clearly a clickable thread-starter, not just commentary.
