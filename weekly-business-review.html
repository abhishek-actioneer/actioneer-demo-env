Revenue Trend
Daily Revenue — Nov 1–7

Revenue peaked on Monday, Nov 4 at $8.03M — a 15% lift above the $7.1M weekly average. The weekend held above $6.4M, suggesting strong organic demand. Wednesday recovered to $7.4M before Thursday's slight pullback to close the week at $7.1M.

Product Mix
Revenue by Category
Smartphones represent 68.7% of total revenue

Smartphones are the overwhelming revenue engine at $34.2M. TVs ($1.8M) and Notebooks ($1.77M) are virtually tied for second. The long tail — Smartwatches, Washers, Headphones — each contributes under $1.1M but serves distinct buyer segments with meaningful volume.

Brand Performance
Top 5 Brands by Revenue

Apple and Samsung together account for 69% of total revenue. Apple leads on revenue ($24.5M) while Samsung leads on order count (37K vs 31.8K) — a premium vs. volume split. Xiaomi, Huawei, and LG form a distant third tier, collectively below $3.5M.

Competitive Snapshot
Apple vs Samsung
Revenue leadership vs order volume

Apple's $771.70 AOV is nearly 3× Samsung's $266.81. Samsung compensates with volume: 37,012 orders vs Apple's 31,811. Despite more transactions, Samsung generates $14.7M less revenue — a stark illustration of how pricing strategy shapes brand economics.

Purchase Behavior
When Customers Buy
Hourly purchase distribution · Nov 1–7 aggregate

Purchase volume peaks between 7–9am UTC, holding near 12,000 orders/hour across the full week. Activity declines steadily through the afternoon and drops sharply after 6pm. Overnight hours are minimal. This strongly favors early-morning campaign timing.

Recommended Actions
What to Do Next
01
Timing

Amplify the Monday lift with Sunday-night priming

Monday Nov 4 revenue was 15% above the weekly average. Schedule email sends and push notifications for Sunday 8–9pm local time to warm buyers before Monday demand peaks. A Sunday flash sale could compound the existing behavioral pattern.

02
Merchandising

Shift premium placements toward Apple — 2.5× the revenue per order

Apple generates $771.70 per order vs Samsung's $266.81. Homepage hero slots, featured collections, and cross-sell recommendations should prioritize Apple products. Every Apple conversion replaces ~3 Samsung conversions in revenue terms.

03
Acquisition

Time campaigns to 6am UTC — arrive before the purchase peak

Purchases peak 7–9am UTC. Email sends and paid retargeting windows should target 6am UTC to reach customers at the start of their buying window, before inbox competition intensifies at peak hours.
