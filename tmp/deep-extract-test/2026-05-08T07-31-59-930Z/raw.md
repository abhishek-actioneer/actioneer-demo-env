# Aptus FY25 Performance Review – Growth Drivers, Risk Signals & Strategic Outlook

## Executive Summary
Aptus Value Housing Finance delivered robust growth in FY25, characterized by a 25% year-on-year expansion in Assets Under Management (AUM) to ₹10,865 crore and a 23% increase in Profit After Tax (PAT) to ₹751 crore [1]. The company successfully scaled its physical footprint to 300 branches while launching a fully digital Loan Origination System (LOS) to drive operational efficiency [1]. Despite this strong top-line performance, rising borrowing costs led to a slight compression in Net Interest Margins (NIM) and Return on Assets (ROA) [1]. Asset quality remains stable, though a marginal uptick in Gross Non-Performing Assets (GNPA) warrants continued monitoring [1]. Moving forward, Aptus is well-positioned to leverage its strong capital base (71% CRAR) and diversified funding mix to deepen market penetration in emerging geographies like Maharashtra and Odisha [1].

## FY25 Financial Performance
Strong top-line growth and profitability are tempered by modest margin compression due to rising borrowing costs.

Aptus demonstrated significant financial momentum in FY25, driven by a 28% increase in total income and a 15% rise in loan disbursements [1]. However, the cost of borrowings increased from 8.55% to 8.68%, which placed downward pressure on margins [1]. Despite this, the company optimized its opex-to-assets ratio to 2.63%, indicating strong operating leverage [1].

| **AUM (₹ Crores)** | 8,722 | 10,865 | +25% |
| **Disbursements (₹ Crores)** | 3,127 | 3,604 | +15% |

*The financial metrics highlight a successful balance of aggressive portfolio expansion and sustained profitability, even as margin pressures emerge from the broader interest rate environment [1].*

## Asset Quality & Risk Management
Asset quality remains solid despite rapid book expansion, supported by disciplined underwriting and in-house collections.

Aptus maintained stable asset quality in FY25, with a provision coverage ratio (PCR) of 25% on NPAs and a steady credit cost of 0.29% [1]. The company's hybrid field- and central credit process sanctions over 90% of proposals within 72 hours, maintaining a rejection rate of 20–22% to ensure credit rigor [1]. 

| Asset Quality Metric | FY24 | FY25 | Change |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Gross NPA (%)** | 1.07% | 1.19% | +12 bps |
| **Net NPA (%)** | 0.80% | 0.89% | +9 bps |
| **Collection Efficiency (%)** | 100.15% | 101.16% | +101 bps |

*While NPAs saw a slight increase, reflecting prudent provisioning amid loan book expansion, the overall collection efficiency improved, underscoring the effectiveness of Aptus's digital collection tools and early warning systems [1].*

## Capital Structure & Liquidity
Capital cushions are ample; diversification of funding reduces concentration risk and supports future scalability.

The company's net worth grew by 15% to ₹4,317 crore, supported by internal accruals and retained earnings [1]. Aptus maintains a highly conservative capital adequacy ratio (CRAR) of 71%, well above regulatory requirements [1]. The funding mix is well-diversified across bank borrowings (52%), NCDs (19%), NHB refinance (15%), and securitisation/other channels (14%) [1].

Aptus expanded its physical footprint significantly in FY25, closing the year with 300 branches across six states and one union territory [1]. The expansion strategy focuses on deepening presence in core southern states while entering adjacent markets with cultural and operational continuity [1].

## Human Capital & Culture
Workforce growth fuels expansion but must be matched with productivity gains and targeted retention strategies.

To support its expanding branch network, Aptus increased its employee base by 433, reaching a total of 3,351 employees in FY25 [1]. The company emphasizes localized recruitment and a "growth-from-within" culture, offering ESOPs to senior and middle managers to ensure strong retention [1].

## Risks & Mitigation
While Aptus exhibits strong fundamentals, the primary risks include margin compression from rising borrowing costs and a slight uptick in GNPA (up 12 bps to 1.19%) [1]. The company mitigates these risks through a balanced funding mix (56% floating, 30% repo-linked) and rigorous credit evaluation models supported by machine learning and over 100 data points [1].

## Recommendations & Action Plan
To sustain its growth trajectory and protect profitability, Aptus should prioritize the following actions:
1. **Accelerate Direct Assignments:** Scale the direct assignment program to the targeted ₹100–150 crore per quarter to lower the overall cost of funds and defend NIMs [1].
2. **Enhance Predictive Analytics:** Deepen the integration of ML-based predictive models to monitor risk and provide early-warning signals, ensuring the slight GNPA uptick does not become a trend [1].
3. **Optimize Branch Productivity:** Implement standardized performance dashboards across the 38 new branches to accelerate their path to maturity and maintain the opex-to-assets ratio below 2.70% [1].
