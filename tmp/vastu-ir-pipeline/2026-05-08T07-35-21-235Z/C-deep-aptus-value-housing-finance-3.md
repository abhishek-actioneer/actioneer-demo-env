# Aptus Value Housing Finance FY23 Competitor Research Report

## Executive Summary

Aptus Value Housing Finance demonstrated exceptional growth and operational efficiency in FY23, solidifying its position in the affordable housing finance sector. The company achieved a 30% year-on-year growth in Assets Under Management (AUM), reaching ₹6,738 Crores [1]. This expansion was driven by a 46% surge in loan disbursements, which totaled ₹2,394 Crores for the fiscal year [1]. 

Strategically, Aptus is prioritizing deep market penetration in southern states while initiating geographical diversification, notably entering Odisha and planning expansion into Maharashtra [1]. The company maintained robust profitability despite interest rate headwinds, sustaining a Return on Assets (ROA) of 8.44% and expanding its Return on Equity (ROE) to 16.34% [1]. Asset quality remains a strong competitive advantage, with Gross Non-Performing Assets (GNPA) improving to 1.15% [1].

## Financial Performance & Profitability

Aptus delivered stellar financial results in FY23, characterized by high growth in both top-line income and bottom-line profitability. The company successfully navigated a challenging macroeconomic environment marked by rising interest rates.

| Financial Metric | FY23 Value | YoY Growth / Context |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Assets Under Management (AUM)** | ₹6,738 Cr | >30% YoY growth [1] |
| **Total Disbursements** | ₹2,394 Cr | 46% YoY increase [1] |
| **Total Income** | ₹1,129 Cr | 34% YoY growth [1] |
| **Profit After Tax (PAT)** | ₹503 Cr | 36% YoY growth [1] |
| **Net Worth** | ₹3,339 Cr | Strong capital base [1] |
| **Return on Assets (ROA)** | 8.44% | Consistent year-on-year [1] |
| **Return on Equity (ROE)** | 16.34% | Increased from 14.45% in FY22 [1] |
| **Spreads (NIM proxy)** | 14.3% | Increased by 65 basis points [1] |

The 36% growth in Profit After Tax outpaced the 34% growth in total income, indicating strong operational leverage and cost management [1]. Furthermore, the ability to expand spreads by 65 basis points to 14.3% during a period of significant interest rate headwinds highlights Aptus's pricing power and effective liability management [1].

## Asset Quality & Risk Management

Aptus maintained stringent credit risk management protocols, resulting in improved asset quality metrics across the board. The company achieved a collection efficiency of over 100%, which directly contributed to a reduction in soft bucket outstanding and overdues [1].

| Asset Quality Metric | FY23 Value | FY22 Value | Trend |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Gross NPA (GNPA)** | 1.15% | 1.19% | Improved [1] |
| **Net NPA (NNPA)** | 0.86% | 0.88% | Improved [1] |
| **30+ Days Past Due (DPD)** | 5.90% | 9.91% | Significant Improvement [1] |
| **Provision Coverage Ratio** | 1.06% | 0.80% | Strengthened [1] |

The sharp reduction in the 30+ DPD rate from 9.91% to 5.90% is a critical leading indicator of portfolio health, suggesting that future NPA formation will likely remain subdued [1]. The company also prudently increased its provision coverage ratio, providing a stronger buffer against potential future credit losses [1].

## Operational Scale & Strategic Expansion

Aptus's growth is underpinned by a deliberate strategy of deep market penetration rather than thin, pan-India expansion. The company focuses on clustering branches to ensure comprehensive carpet coverage, aiming for a branch every 70 to 80 kilometers in its core operating states [1].

### Network and Customer Base Expansion

During FY23, Aptus expanded its physical footprint and customer reach significantly:
* **Branch Network:** Added 23 new branches, bringing the total network to 231 branches across Tamil Nadu, Andhra Pradesh, Telangana, Karnataka, and Odisha [1].
* **Customer Base:** The active customer base grew by 28% year-on-year, exceeding 107,000 live customers [1].
* **Human Capital:** The company expanded its workforce to 2,405 employees to support its growing operations [1].

### Geographic Diversification

While maintaining its stronghold in the South, Aptus has initiated strategic geographic diversification. The company successfully commenced operations in Odisha and is actively planning to add new branches in Maharashtra [1]. This targeted expansion allows Aptus to tap into new growth markets while applying its proven, localized underwriting and collection models.

## Management Discussion & Analysis (MD&A) Highlights

The management's narrative for FY23 centers on execution, resilience, and sustainable growth. Key strategic takeaways from the MD&A include:

* **Robust Portfolio Expansion:** AUM grew by over 30% to ₹6,738 Crores, driven by a 46% surge in disbursements (₹2,394 Crores), underscoring strong demand in the affordable housing segment [1].
* **Exceptional Capital Utilization:** Management emphasized the company's industry-leading return metrics, with ROA holding steady at 8.44% and ROE rising to 16.34%, reflecting highly efficient capital allocation [1].
* **Asset Quality Resilience:** Despite macroeconomic pressures, proactive collection efforts improved asset quality, bringing Gross NPA down to 1.15% and Net NPA to 0.86% [1].
* **Strategic Network Growth:** The addition of 23 new branches (totaling 231) aligns with the company's strategy of deep market penetration and localized customer engagement [1].
