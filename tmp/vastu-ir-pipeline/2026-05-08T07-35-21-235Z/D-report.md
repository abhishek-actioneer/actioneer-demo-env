## Where competitors are ahead
* **Highest Profitability (PAT):** **Aptus** leads the peer group with a Profit After Tax of ₹751 Cr in FY25 [7], outperforming Aavas (₹574.11 Cr in FY25) [5] and Home First (₹540 Cr in FY26) [10].
* **Superior Profit Growth:** **Home First** reported the highest bottom-line momentum with a PAT YoY growth of 41.4% in FY26 [10], significantly higher than India Shelter's 33% [15] and Aptus's 23% [7].
* **Best-in-Class Capital Adequacy:** **Aptus** maintains a formidable Capital Risk Adequacy Ratio (CRAR) of 71% [7], providing a much larger buffer than India Shelter (55.8%) [15], Aavas (44.5%) [5], and Home First (44.1%) [10].
* **Widest Geographic Reach:** **India Shelter** possesses the largest physical network with 307 branches as of March 2026 [15], compared to Aptus (300) [7] and Home First (171) [10].
* **Highest Portfolio Yields (NIM):** **Aptus** and **India Shelter** both maintain industry-leading Net Interest Margins (NIM) at 12.96% [7] and 12.5% [15] respectively, whereas Home First reported a NIM of 5.7% [10].

## Recent strategic moves (last 6 months)

**Aavas Financiers**
* **Ownership Change:** Aquilo House Pte. Ltd. completed the acquisition of a 48.96% controlling stake in the company, marking a transition to a new promoter group [4].
* **Capital Market Activity:** Raised ₹1,030 Crore through Private Placement of Non-Convertible Debentures (NCDs) between October 2024 and June 2025 to fund its disbursement pipeline [4].

**Aptus Value Housing Finance**
* **Digital Transformation:** Launched a mobile-first, fully digital Lead Origination System (LOS) in April 2024 to streamline customer onboarding [7].
* **Geographic Expansion:** Expanded into new regional markets by opening 10 branches across Maharashtra and Odisha during FY25 [7].

**Home First Finance**
* **Major Capital Raise:** Successfully raised ₹1,250 Crore through a Qualified Institutional Placement (QIP) in April 2026, significantly strengthening its net worth to ₹4,357 Crore [10].
* **Sustainability Funding:** Secured a ₹280 Crore (₹2,800 Mn) NCD investment from the IFC to focus on green financing and sustainable housing [10].

**India Shelter Finance**
* **Network Growth:** Added 41 new branches during FY26, bringing the total locations to 307 [15].
* **Green Certification:** Partnered with the IFC under the EDGE Programme to collaborate on Green Housing Certification [15].
