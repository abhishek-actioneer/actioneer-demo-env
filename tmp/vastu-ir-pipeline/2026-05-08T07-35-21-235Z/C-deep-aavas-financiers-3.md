# Aavas Financiers FY24 Performance Review & Strategic Outlook

## 1. Executive Summary

Aavas Financiers demonstrated robust growth and stable asset quality in FY24, balancing aggressive loan book expansion with disciplined risk management. The company's financial results highlight significant momentum in core lending operations, though operational cash outflows suggest a need for optimized working capital management. 

Key strategic insights include:
* **Robust Loan Portfolio Expansion**: Loans grew to ₹14,004.37 Cr (14,00,436.68 lakh) versus ₹11,476.27 Cr a year earlier [1]. The company should prioritize scaling credit underwriting capacity while tightening risk-grade monitoring.
* **Strong Profitability Momentum**: Profit After Tax (PAT) for the year reached ₹490.69 Cr (49,069.42 lakh), up from ₹430.07 Cr in FY23 [1]. 
* **Low Asset Quality Amid Growth**: Gross NPA (GNPA) stayed at 0.94% and Net NPA (NNPA) at 0.67% [1]. 
* **Solid Capital Cushion**: The Capital Risk Adequacy Ratio (CRAR) was reported at 43.99%, well above regulatory minimums [1].
* **Operational Cash Flow Pressure**: Net cash used in operating activities was -₹1,98,659.13 lakh, with the end-of-year cash position falling to ₹2,055.80 lakh [1].

## 2. Financial Performance Overview

The FY24 financial results reflect a strong top-line and bottom-line trajectory, driven primarily by core interest income and effective cost management.

### 2.1 Assets Under Management & Loan Book

The company's loan book expanded significantly, reaching ₹14,004.37 Cr (14,00,436.68 lakh) by the end of FY24, compared to ₹11,476.27 Cr (11,47,627.45 lakh) in FY23 [1]. This growth underscores Aavas's successful penetration in its target housing finance markets.

### 2.2 Income Statement Highlights

Total income for FY24 stood at ₹2,020.30 Cr (2,02,029.64 lakh), a substantial increase from ₹1,610.15 Cr (1,61,014.56 lakh) in the previous year [1]. Interest income remains the dominant revenue driver, contributing ₹1,734.65 Cr (1,73,465.04 lakh) [1].

## 3. Asset Quality & Risk Profile

Despite the rapid expansion of the loan portfolio, Aavas Financiers has maintained strict control over its asset quality, a critical indicator of sustainable growth in the housing finance sector.

### 3.1 NPA Trends & Drivers

Asset quality metrics remained highly stable. The Gross Non-Performing Assets (GNPA) ratio stood at 0.94%, and the Net Non-Performing Assets (NNPA) ratio was 0.67% [1]. The provision coverage ratio was reported at 64.33% [1].

### 3.2 Capital Adequacy & Liquidity

The company is exceptionally well-capitalized to fund its next phase of expansion. The Capital Risk Adequacy Ratio (CRAR) is 43.99%, and the Liquidity Coverage Ratio (LCR) is 106.14% [1]. 

## 4. Capital Structure & Funding Strategy

Aavas's balance sheet reflects a conservative yet growth-oriented capital structure, utilizing a mix of equity, debt securities, and strategic employee incentives.

### 4.1 Debt Composition & Cost

The company's net worth at the end of FY24 was ₹3,773.32 Cr (3,77,331.52 lakh) [1]. The debt-to-equity ratio stands at 3.27, indicating a balanced leverage approach [1]. Borrowings (other than debt securities) totaled ₹10,932.98 Cr (10,93,298.26 lakh) [1].

### 4.2 Equity Actions & ESOP

To align employee interests with long-term value creation, Aavas allotted 35,302 equity shares at INR 215.25 and 47,529 equity shares at INR 328.00 to eligible employees under its Employee Stock Option Plan during FY24 [1].

## 5. Operational Efficiency & Cash Flow

While profitability is strong, the cash flow statement reveals the working capital intensity of the company's rapid loan disbursement strategy.

### 5.1 Cash Flow Statement Deep-Dive

Net cash used in operating activities was significant at -₹1,98,659.13 lakh, primarily driven by the increase in loans (-₹2,54,636.13 lakh) [1]. This was offset by net cash from financing activities totaling ₹2,47,728.37 lakh, largely from proceeds from borrowings [1]. Cash and cash equivalents at the end of the year stood at ₹2,055.80 lakh [1].

## 6. Strategic Moves & Growth Initiatives

Management has actively utilized capital markets and portfolio management tactics to optimize the balance sheet and secure funding.

### 6.1 Capital Raising & Funding

Aavas maintained at least 100% security cover on its secured listed non-convertible debentures (NCDs) as of March 31, 2024, by creating an exclusive charge on specific book debts and receivables [1]. 

### 6.2 Portfolio Management Tactics

During FY24, the company executed significant loan assignments, transferring 16,934 loans amounting to ₹1,38,107 lakh [1]. The company retained a 10% beneficial economic interest (MRR) and maintained 100% tangible security coverage on these assigned loans [1].
