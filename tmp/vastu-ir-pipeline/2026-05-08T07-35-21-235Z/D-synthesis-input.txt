# Aavas Financiers Limited Q1 FY26 Performance & Strategic Outlook – Profitability Surge, Capital-Market Moves, and Asset-Quality Strength

## Executive Summary

Aavas Financiers Limited delivered a robust performance for the quarter ended June 30, 2025 (Q1 FY26), characterized by strong profitability, exceptional asset quality, and a pivotal shift in corporate ownership [1]. The company reported a Profit After Tax (PAT) of ₹139.23 Crore (13,923.34 lakh), up from ₹126.10 Crore in the corresponding quarter of the previous year, maintaining a high Net Profit Margin of 22.17% [1]. Asset quality remains a key strength, with Gross Non-Performing Assets (GNPA) at 1.22% and Net Non-Performing Assets (NNPA) at 0.84% [1]. 

Strategically, the most significant development is the acquisition of a 48.96% controlling stake by Aquilo House Pte. Ltd., marking a transition from the previous promoter group [1]. The company is exceptionally well-capitalized, boasting a Capital Risk Adequacy Ratio (CRAR) of 43.20%, far exceeding regulatory requirements, and a robust Liquidity Coverage Ratio of 128.28% [1]. With a net worth of ₹4,509.81 Crore (4,50,980.69 lakh) and active expansion of its co-lending and loan assignment programs, Aavas is well-positioned to leverage its strong balance sheet for accelerated growth in the housing finance sector [1].

Aavas Financiers demonstrated solid top-line and bottom-line growth during the quarter ended June 30, 2025. Total income reached ₹627.93 Crore (62,793.49 lakh), compared to ₹542.55 Crore (54,255.50 lakh) in the same quarter the previous year [1]. This growth was primarily driven by interest income, which stood at ₹548.94 Crore (54,894.10 lakh) [1]. The company's focus on operational efficiency and margin preservation resulted in a strong Profit Before Tax of ₹179.12 Crore (17,912.86 lakh) and a PAT of ₹139.23 Crore [1].

# Aptus Value Housing Finance FY24: Growth, Profitability, and Expansion Insights

## Executive Summary

Aptus Value Housing Finance delivered exceptional double-digit growth across its assets, earnings, and operational reach in FY24, solidifying its position in the affordable housing finance sector. The company's Assets Under Management (AUM) surged to ₹8,722 crores, representing a robust 29% year-on-year increase [1]. This growth was fueled by a 31% jump in loan disbursements, which reached ₹3,127 crores [1]. Profitability mirrored this expansion, with Profit After Tax (PAT) climbing 22% to ₹612 crores on the back of ₹1,417 crores in total income [1]. 

Crucially, Aptus achieved this rapid expansion while improving its asset quality. Gross Non-Performing Assets (GNPA) improved to 1.07%, and Net Non-Performing Assets (NNPA) fell to 0.80% [1]. The company maintains a formidable capital buffer, with a Capital Adequacy Ratio (CRAR) of 73.03%, well above regulatory requirements [1]. With the addition of 31 new branches and a 25% increase in its customer base to 133,499, Aptus has established a strong platform for sustained, profitable growth in underserved Tier-2, Tier-3, and Tier-4 markets [1].

## Financial Performance

Aptus demonstrated strong top-line and bottom-line momentum in FY24, driven by efficient operations and a broadening customer base. Total income grew by 25% to ₹1,417 crores, up from ₹1,134 crores in FY23 [1]. This revenue expansion was primarily supported by a 25% increase in interest income, which reached ₹1,320 crores, alongside fee and other income growth from ₹75 crores to ₹97 crores [1]. 

The company's profitability metrics remain highly attractive. Profit Before Tax (PBT) increased by 21% to ₹793 crores, while PAT grew by 22% to ₹612 crores [1]. This earnings growth translated into strong shareholder returns, with Return on Equity (ROE) improving to 17.25% from 16.34% in the previous year, and Return on Assets (ROA) remaining robust at 8.00% [1].

| Metric | FY23 | FY24 | YoY Growth |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |

| **Profit After Tax (PAT)** | ₹503 cr | ₹612 cr | 22% |
| **Net Worth** | ₹3,339 cr | ₹3,768 cr | 13% |

*The 29% AUM growth and 31% disbursement surge highlight Aptus's successful market penetration, while the 22% PAT growth confirms the scalability and profitability of its lending model [1].*

## Asset Quality & Risk Metrics

Maintaining a high-quality loan book remains a core priority for Aptus, and FY24 results indicate highly effective credit risk management despite rapid portfolio growth. The company successfully reduced its non-performing assets, with the GNPA ratio improving to 1.07% (down from 1.15% in FY23) and the NNPA ratio improving to 0.80% (down from 0.86%) [1]. 

These improvements are attributed to stringent credit evaluation processes, robust risk management practices, and highly effective collection strategies [1]. Notably, Aptus achieved a remarkable collection efficiency of 100.15% in Q4 FY24, underscoring the strength of its collections infrastructure [1]. The Net Interest Margin (NIM) remained healthy at 13.45%, reflecting efficient asset-liability management [1].

A key milestone in this expansion was the opening of its first branch in Maharashtra, signaling a strategic push into new regional markets [1].

## Capital Structure & Adequacy

Aptus maintains a fortress balance sheet that provides substantial headroom for future growth. The company's net worth increased by 13% to ₹3,768 crores in FY24, up from ₹3,339 crores in FY23 [1]. To fund its expanding loan portfolio, borrowings rose to ₹5,200 crores from ₹3,786 crores, resulting in a debt-to-equity ratio of 1.38:1 (up from 1.14:1 in FY23) [1].

Aptus's core mission of serving low- and middle-income groups, self-employed individuals, and small business owners yielded significant customer growth in FY24. The total customer base expanded by 25% to reach 133,499 customers [1]. # Aptus Value Housing Finance FY23 Competitor Research Report

## Executive Summary

Aptus Value Housing Finance demonstrated exceptional growth and operational efficiency in FY23, solidifying its position in the affordable housing finance sector. The company achieved a 30% year-on-year growth in Assets Under Management (AUM), reaching ₹6,738 Crores [1]. This expansion was driven by a 46% surge in loan disbursements, which totaled ₹2,394 Crores for the fiscal year [1]. 

Strategically, Aptus is prioritizing deep market penetration in southern states while initiating geographical diversification, notably entering Odisha and planning expansion into Maharashtra [1]. The company maintained robust profitability despite interest rate headwinds, sustaining a Return on Assets (ROA) of 8.44% and expanding its Return on Equity (ROE) to 16.34% [1]. Asset quality remains a strong competitive advantage, with Gross Non-Performing Assets (GNPA) improving to 1.15% [1].

## Financial Performance & Profitability

Aptus delivered stellar financial results in FY23, characterized by high growth in both top-line income and bottom-line profitability. The company successfully navigated a challenging macroeconomic environment marked by rising interest rates.

## Operational Scale & Strategic Expansion

This targeted expansion allows Aptus to tap into new growth markets while applying its proven, localized underwriting and collection models.

# Home First FY 25 Competitive Snapshot: Growth Drivers, Profitability Upside & Capital-Strength Risks

## Executive Summary

Home First Finance Company India Ltd. delivered a robust financial and operational performance in FY25, characterized by significant portfolio expansion and sustained profitability. Assets Under Management (AUM) surged by 31.1% year-over-year to reach ₹12,712.72 Crores, driven by a 21.2% increase in annual disbursements to ₹4,805 Crores [1]. Despite this rapid growth, the company maintained stable asset quality, with Gross Non-Performing Assets (GNPA) holding steady at 1.7% and Net Non-Performing Assets (NNPA) at 1.3% [1]. 

Profitability metrics remained exceptionally strong, with Profit After Tax (PAT) growing 25.0% to ₹382.07 Crores, yielding a Return on Equity (RoE) of 16.5% [1]. The company's capital position was further fortified post-FY25 through a ₹1,250 Crore Qualified Institutional Placement (QIP) in April 2025, pushing the Capital Adequacy Ratio (CRAR) to a highly robust 32.8% [1]. Operationally, Home First expanded its physical footprint to 155 branches and 361 touchpoints, serving an active customer base of 117,989, of which approximately 89% are women [1]. The strategic imperative moving forward is to leverage this excess capital and expanded network to capture further market share while maintaining stringent cost controls and underwriting discipline.
