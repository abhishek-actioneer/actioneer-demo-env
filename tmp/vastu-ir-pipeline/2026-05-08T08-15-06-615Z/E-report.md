- A strong capital position, evidenced by a Capital Risk Adequacy Ratio of 46.42% as of September 30, 2025, provides a solid foundation for growth. [↗](https://www.aavas.in/uploads/pdf/outcome-46562990.pdf)
- Aavas Financiers focuses solely on the Indian housing finance market, reporting no other distinct operating segments. [↗](https://www.aavas.in/uploads/pdf/outcome-of-board-meetingq4-42612506.pdf)
- Asset quality is maintained at healthy levels, with Gross Non-Performing Assets reported at 1.24% as of September 30, 2025. [↗](https://www.aavas.in/uploads/pdf/outcome-46562990.pdf)
- The company maintains a strong liquidity buffer, with a Liquidity Coverage Ratio of 142.90% as of September 2025. [↗](https://www.aavas.in/uploads/pdf/outcome-46562990.pdf)

### Aptus Value Housing Finance

- Aggressive distribution expansion added 16 branches and 12 touchpoints in FY26, reaching a total of 171 branches across India. [↗](https://homefirstindia.com/files/HomeFirst_Q4FY26_Press_Release.pdf)

- The core product segment, comprising 83% of the total loan portfolio as of March 2026. [↗](https://homefirstindia.com/files/Investor%20Deck%20Q4FY26.pdf)
- Represents the largest portion of the customer base, providing portfolio stability with 67.9% of AUM. [↗](https://homefirstindia.com/files/Integrated%20Annual%20Report%20FY25.pdf)
- Management maintains a stable credit cost outlook of 30 to 40 basis points despite scaling operations. [↗](https://homefirstindia.com/files/HomeFirst_Q4FY26_Press_Release.pdf)

### India Shelter Finance
- Sustained demand in affordable housing and expansion into Tier 2 and 3 cities drive growth, with AUM reaching ₹11,044 Crs in FY26. [↗](https://india-shelter.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/uploads/1751527210017-Integrated+Annual+Report-+2024-25.pdf)
- High digital adoption, including 94% Account Aggregator penetration and 73% digital collections, enhances operational efficiency and scalability. [↗](https://india-shelter.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/uploads/1777716210890-Investor%20Presentation%20intimation%20%283%29.pdf)
- Government schemes like PMAY, with an ₹807 Bn allocation in 2024-25, continue to accelerate affordable housing demand. [↗](https://india-shelter.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/uploads/1751527210017-Integrated+Annual+Report-+2024-25.pdf)
- LAP has grown at a 32% CAGR over three years, providing vital liquidity to MSMEs, though growth is projected to stabilize. [↗](https://india-shelter.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/uploads/1751527210017-Integrated+Annual+Report-+2024-25.pdf)
- Core segment comprising 67% of the book, specifically targeting first-time buyers in low- and middle-income segments. [↗](https://india-shelter.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/uploads/1777716210890-Investor%20Presentation%20intimation%20%283%29.pdf)
- Growth in the Loan Against Property (LAP) segment is expected to normalize to the 22-23% range in the coming years. [↗](https://india-shelter.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/uploads/1751527210017-Integrated+Annual+Report-+2024-25.pdf)
