**Geographic Presence:** The Company has a
diversified presence in India, spread across 14
states, with no state contributing more than 15%
of the total AUM. In FY24, the Company added 27
branches, taking its consolidated branch network
to 206 branches from 179 in FY23.
▪
**Portfolio:** The Company’s AUM crossed the ₹7,000
crore milestone in FY24, growing 40% YoY to
₹7,419 crore from ₹5,293 crore in FY23. Housing
loans grew faster (49% YoY) than non-housing
loans (27% YoY), contributing 63% of AUM as
against 59% in the previous year. On-book loans
grew 38% YoY to ₹6,067 crore in FY24 from 4,400
crore in FY23.
▪
**Disbursements:** Loan disbursements in FY24
grew 22% YoY to ₹3,548 crore as against ₹2,909
crore in FY23, led by focus on housing loans (grew
36% YoY). Housing loans comprised 69% of the
disbursements in FY24 compared to 62% in the
previous fiscal.
