**FY25 Key Financial and Operational**
**Highlights**
**Assets Under Management (AUM)**
AUM grew 42% YoY to ₹2,435 crore in FY25 from
₹1,717 crore in FY24, reflecting strong demand and deeper
market penetration.
**Disbursements**
Disbursements totalled ₹1,583 crore in FY25, growing 12%
YoY, despite macroeconomic headwinds in select markets.
**Net Interest Income (NII)**
NII increased 60% YoY to ₹157 crore in FY25 (from ₹98 crore
in FY24), in line with loan book expansion and improved
interest spreads.
**Securitisation**
Total securitisation reduced to ₹188 crore in FY25 (from ₹355
crore in FY24), resulting in a net loss of ₹9 crore from Direct
Assignment transactions, compared to ₹19 crore net income
in FY24.
