## Where Vastu is ahead

*   **Superior Historical Asset Quality:** In FY24, Vastu maintained a Gross Stage 3 asset ratio of 0.89%, which was the lowest among the peer group, outperforming Aptus (1.07%), India Shelter (1.0%), and Home First (1.7%) ([Vastu AR 23-24](https://bmkzz2nd6igg.compat.objectstorage.ap-mumbai-1.oraclecloud.com/hfc-g-dc-vastu-website/uploads/public/assets/pdf/annual-reports/annual-report-23-24.pdf?X-Amz-Content-Sha256=UNSIGNED-PAYLOAD&X-Amz-Algorithm=AWS4-HMAC-SHA256&X-Amz-Credential=48ce50f1665892d0f174106dd175f7c1f6b9f25a%2F20260508%2Fap-mumbai-1%2Fs3%2Faws4_request&X-Amz-Date=20260508T071225Z&X-Amz-SignedHeaders=host&X-Amz-Expires=7200&X-Amz-Signature=d7c42aa1fff46e1b14ca64a62c2935bc91b577d5b28946641e709398206fe127)).
*   **High Capital Adequacy:** Vastu reported a CRAR of 63.8% in FY24, significantly higher than peers like India Shelter (60.6% in FY25) and Aavas (44.5% in FY25), providing a massive buffer for future growth ([Vastu AR 23-24](https://bmkzz2nd6igg.compat.objectstorage.ap-mumbai-1.oraclecloud.com/hfc-g-dc-vastu-website/uploads/public/assets/pdf/annual-reports/annual-report-23-24.pdf?X-Amz-Content-Sha256=UNSIGNED-PAYLOAD&X-Amz-Algorithm=AWS4-HMAC-SHA256&X-Amz-Credential=48ce50f1665892d0f174106dd175f7c1f6b9f25a%2F20260508%2Fap-mumbai-1%2Fs3%2Faws4_request&X-Amz-Date=20260508T071225Z&X-Amz-SignedHeaders=host&X-Amz-Expires=7200&X-Amz-Signature=d7c42aa1fff46e1b14ca64a62c2935bc91b577d5b28946641e709398206fe127)).

## Where competitors are ahead

*   **Profitability Metrics (RoA/RoE):** Aptus leads the segment with an RoA of 7.73% and RoE of 18.76% for FY25, substantially higher than Vastu’s 3.2% RoA and 11.5% Return on Net Worth for the same period ([Aptus AR 24-25](https://aptusindia.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/Aptus_AR_Mar+2025.pdf)).
*   **AUM Growth Momentum:** India Shelter and Home First achieved AUM growth of 31.5% and 31.1% respectively in FY25, outpacing Vastu’s consolidated growth of 25% ([Home First AR 24-25](https://homefirstindia.com/files/Integrated%20Annual%20Report%20FY25.pdf); [India Shelter AR 24-25](https://india-shelter.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/uploads/1751527210017-Integrated+Annual+Report-+2024-25.pdf)).
*   **Geographic Distribution Network:** Aptus operates 300 branches and India Shelter operates 266 branches as of FY25, compared to Vastu's 226 branches ([Aptus AR 24-25](https://aptusindia.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/Aptus_AR_Mar+2025.pdf); [India Shelter AR 24-25](https://india-shelter.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/uploads/1751527210017-Integrated+Annual+Report-+2024-25.pdf)).
*   **Net Profit Resilience:** While Vastu’s consolidated PAT declined by 10% in FY25 (impacted by lower Direct Assignment income), India Shelter’s PAT grew by 53% to ₹378 crore ([Vastu AR 24-25](https://bmkzz2nd6igg.compat.objectstorage.ap-mumbai-1.oraclecloud.com/hfc-g-dc-vastu-website/uploads/public/assets/pdf/annual-reports/annual-report-24-25.pdf?X-Amz-Content-Sha256=UNSIGNED-PAYLOAD&X-Amz-Algorithm=AWS4-HMAC-SHA256&X-Amz-Credential=48ce50f1665892d0f174106dd175f7c1f6b9f25a%2F20260508%2Fap-mumbai-1%2Fs3%2Faws4_request&X-Amz-Date=20260508T071225Z&X-Amz-SignedHeaders=host&X-Amz-Expires=7200&X-Amz-Signature=57fe361a6f83ca09738f9ccd55cc7011e2c1c7611d01e10484aedbf5638d801a); [India Shelter AR 24-25](https://india-shelter.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/uploads/1751527210017-Integrated+Annual+Report-+2024-25.pdf)).

## Recent strategic moves (last 6 months)

**Vastu Housing Finance**
*   **Equity Infusion:** Welcomed IFC (World Bank Group) with a $40 million equity investment and received a primary equity capital infusion of ₹620 crore in March 2024 ([Vastu AR 23-24](https://bmkzz2nd6igg.compat.objectstorage.ap-mumbai-1.oraclecloud.com/hfc-g-dc-vastu-website/uploads/public/assets/pdf/annual-reports/annual-report-23-24.pdf?X-Amz-Content-Sha256=UNSIGNED-PAYLOAD&X-Amz-Algorithm=AWS4-HMAC-SHA256&X-Amz-Credential=48ce50f1665892d0f174106dd175f7c1f6b9f25a%2F20260508%2Fap-mumbai-1%2Fs3%2Faws4_request&X-Amz-Date=20260508T071225Z&X-Amz-SignedHeaders=host&X-Amz-Expires=7200&X-Amz-Signature=d7c42aa1fff46e1b14ca64a62c2935bc91b577d5b28946641e709398206fe127)).
*   **Product Diversification:** Launched Micro-housing and MSME offerings, now operational in 128 branches with over ₹1,100 crore in combined AUM ([Vastu AR 24-25](https://bmkzz2nd6igg.compat.objectstorage.ap-mumbai-1.oraclecloud.com/hfc-g-dc-vastu-website/uploads/public/assets/pdf/annual-reports/annual-report-24-25.pdf?X-Amz-Content-Sha256=UNSIGNED-PAYLOAD&X-Amz-Algorithm=AWS4-HMAC-SHA256&X-Amz-Credential=48ce50f1665892d0f174106dd175f7c1f6b9f25a%2F20260508%2Fap-mumbai-1%2Fs3%2Faws4_request&X-Amz-Date=20260508T071225Z&X-Amz-SignedHeaders=host&X-Amz-Expires=7200&X-Amz-Signature=57fe361a6f83ca09738f9ccd55cc7011e2c1c7611d01e10484aedbf5638d801a)).

**Aptus Value Housing Finance**
*   **Geographic Expansion:** Entered the Maharashtra and Odisha markets, adding 10 branches in these new territories during FY25 ([Aptus AR 24-25](https://aptusindia.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/Aptus_AR_Mar+2025.pdf)).
*   **Staffing:** Increased staff strength to 3,351 employees, a YoY addition of 433, to support branch productivity ([Aptus AR 24-25](https://aptusindia.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/Aptus_AR_Mar+2025.pdf)).

**Home First Finance**
*   **Capital Raising:** Successfully completed a Qualified Institutional Placement (QIP) in April 2025, raising ₹1,250 crore ([Home First AR 24-25](https://homefirstindia.com/files/Integrated%20Annual%20Report%20FY25.pdf)).
*   **Digital Distribution:** Expanded to 373 total touchpoints (171 branches + 202 digital touchpoints) as of March 2026 ([Home First Q4FY26 Press Release](https://homefirstindia.com/files/HomeFirst_Q4FY26_Press_Release.pdf)).

**India Shelter Finance**
*   **In-House Sourcing:** Maintained a 99% in-house sourcing model across 307 branches as of March 2026 ([India Shelter Investor Presentation Mar 2026](https://india-shelter.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/uploads/1777716210890-Investor%20Presentation%20intimation%20%283%29.pdf)).
*   **Sustainability:** Certified 25,000 trees planted and transitioned to 95% digital collections ([India Shelter AR 24-25](https://india-shelter.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/uploads/1751527210017-Integrated+Annual+Report-+2024-25.pdf)).

**Aavas Financiers**
*   **Corporate Restructuring:** Aavas Finserv Limited, its erstwhile subsidiary, was dissolved following an NCLT order dated January 28, 2025 ([Aavas Q4 FY25 Results](https://www.aavas.in/uploads/pdf/outcome-of-board-meetingq4-42612506.pdf)).
*   **Stake Sale:** Partners Group and other major stakeholders entered share sale agreements in mid-2025 ([Aavas Q1 FY26 Results](https://www.aavas.in/uploads/pdf/financials-85794283.pdf)).
