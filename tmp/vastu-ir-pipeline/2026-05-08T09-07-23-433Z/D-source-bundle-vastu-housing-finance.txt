will promote increased prudence and stronger capital
buffers. Given the pivotal role HFCs play in credit
outreach, the focus and commitment of both regulators

weight circular excluded housing loans. HFCs are getting
preference over NBFCs, as can be seen from the liquidity
and funding angle.

thrust from the government. Vastu is well-positioned to
