- High capital solvency with a Capital Risk Adequacy Ratio (CRAR) of 44.50% providing a strong foundation for book growth. [↗](https://www.aavas.in/uploads/pdf/outcome-of-board-meetingq4-42612506.pdf)
- Stable asset quality demonstrated by a Gross Non-Performing Assets (GNPA) ratio of 1.08% and Net NPA of 0.73%. [↗](https://www.aavas.in/uploads/pdf/outcome-of-board-meetingq4-42612506.pdf)

### Aptus Value Housing Finance
- Strategic focus on low- and middle-income segments in Tier-3 and Tier-4 cities allows Aptus to tap into large, underserved markets where traditional competition is limited. [↗](https://aptusindia.s3.amazonaws.com/Aptus+Value+Housing+Finance+India+Ltd_Annual+Report+FY24.pdf)
- Digital transformation is expanding reach, with 18% of business in Q4 FY24 originating from digital channels including customer referral and construction ecosystem apps. [↗](https://aptusindia.s3.amazonaws.com/Aptus+Value+Housing+Finance+India+Ltd_Annual+Report+FY24.pdf)
- Home loan AUM is projected to grow by 24% in the next fiscal year, aided by revamped interest subsidy schemes and potential easing of interest rates. [↗](https://aptusindia.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/Aptus_AR_Mar+2025.pdf)
- LAP growth is expected to moderate to 22-23% due to regulatory constraints such as Principal Business Criteria (PBC) compliance and monthly reporting norms. [↗](https://aptusindia.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/Aptus_AR_Mar+2025.pdf)
- The company expects assets under management (AUM) growth of 22–23% for the current and subsequent fiscal years, a recalibration from previous 31% growth rates. [↗](https://aptusindia.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/Aptus_AR_Mar+2025.pdf)
- Return on Managed Assets (RoMA) is projected to remain healthy at approximately 2.4% by fiscal 2026 despite potential yield compression. [↗](https://aptusindia.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/Aptus_AR_Mar+2025.pdf)

### Home First Finance
- Assets Under Management (AUM) grew 24.9% YoY to ₹15,878 crore in Q4 FY26, driven by record disbursements and sustained business momentum. [↗](https://homefirstindia.com/files/Investor%20Deck%20Q4FY26.pdf)
- Aggressive distribution expansion through the addition of 16 branches and 12 touchpoints in FY26, totaling 171 branches and 373 touchpoints. [↗](https://homefirstindia.com/files/Investor%20Deck%20Q4FY26.pdf)
- Strategic partnership with IFC for 'Green Homes' focuses on energy-efficient housing, with 450 homes certified as of March 2026. [↗](https://homefirstindia.com/files/Investor%20Deck%20Q4FY26.pdf)
- Growth is supported by a large market opportunity in affordable housing, specifically targeting ticket sizes between ₹5-25 Lakhs. [↗](https://homefirstindia.com/files/Annual%20Report%202023-24.pdf)
- Core housing loans remain the primary product focus, accounting for 83% of the total AUM as of March 2026. [↗](https://homefirstindia.com/files/Investor%20Deck%20Q4FY26.pdf)
- LAP constitutes 16% of the portfolio, providing diversification while maintaining a focus on secured lending products. [↗](https://homefirstindia.com/files/Investor%20Deck%20Q4FY26.pdf)

### India Shelter Finance

- Management aims to deepen penetration in existing and adjacent markets while improving branch productivity and scaling co-lending opportunities. [↗](https://india-shelter.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/uploads/1777716210890-Investor%20Presentation%20intimation%20%283%29.pdf)

### Aavas Financiers
- Gross Non-Performing Assets (GNPA) have shown a consistent upward trend, rising from 0.94% in March 2024 to 1.22% by June 2025. [↗](https://www.aavas.in/uploads/pdf/financials-85794283.pdf)
- The Provision Coverage Ratio (PCR) has decreased significantly over consecutive quarters, falling from 64.33% to 57.46%, reducing the buffer against potential loan defaults. [↗](https://www.aavas.in/uploads/pdf/financials-85794283.pdf)
- Net Non-Performing Assets (NNPA) have deteriorated, increasing to 0.84% in June 2025 compared to 0.67% in March 2024. [↗](https://www.aavas.in/uploads/pdf/financials-85794283.pdf)
- Residual exposure remains in personal loan accounts restructured under standard resolution plans, totaling over INR 7,341 lakh. [↗](https://www.aavas.in/uploads/pdf/finaloutcome25042024-1252982600.pdf)
- Net Profit Margins have experienced compression, dropping from 24.34% in March 2025 to 22.17% in June 2025. [↗](https://www.aavas.in/uploads/pdf/financials-85794283.pdf)

- Fluctuations in interest rates significantly affect borrowing costs and housing loan demand from sensitive customer segments. [↗](https://aptusindia.s3.amazonaws.com/Aptus+Value+Housing+Finance+India+Ltd_Annual+Report+FY24.pdf)
