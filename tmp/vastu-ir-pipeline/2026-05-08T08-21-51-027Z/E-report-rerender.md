### Aavas Financiers
- Strong capital adequacy provides a foundation for growth, with the Capital Risk Adequacy Ratio (CRAR) reaching 46.42% as of September 2025. [↗](https://www.aavas.in/uploads/pdf/outcome-46562990.pdf)
- The company maintained a healthy net profit margin of 24.34% for the fiscal year ended March 31, 2025. [↗](https://www.aavas.in/uploads/pdf/outcome-of-board-meetingq4-42612506.pdf)
- Liquidity position remains robust with a Liquidity Coverage Ratio of 135.59% as of September 30, 2025, well above regulatory requirements. [↗](https://www.aavas.in/uploads/pdf/outcome-46562990.pdf)
- The company operates exclusively in the housing finance sector within India, with no other reportable business segments. [↗](https://www.aavas.in/uploads/pdf/financials-85794283.pdf)
- Asset quality experienced a slight deterioration, with Gross Non-Performing Assets (GNPA) increasing to 1.24% in September 2025 from 1.08% in March 2025. [↗](https://www.aavas.in/uploads/pdf/outcome-46562990.pdf)
- The company maintains a 100% security cover on its secured listed non-convertible debentures through the hypothecation of specific book debts. [↗](https://www.aavas.in/uploads/pdf/outcome-46562990.pdf)

### Aptus Value Housing Finance
- Strategic focus on low- and middle-income segments in Tier-3 and Tier-4 cities allows Aptus to tap into large underserved markets with deep regional penetration. [↗](https://aptusindia.s3.amazonaws.com/Aptus+Value+Housing+Finance+India+Ltd_Annual+Report+FY24.pdf)
- Increasing digital adoption, with 18% of Q4 FY24 business originating from digital channels like the customer referral and construction ecosystem apps. [↗](https://aptusindia.s3.amazonaws.com/Aptus+Value+Housing+Finance+India+Ltd_Annual+Report+FY24.pdf)
- Asset quality remains high with GNPA improving to 1.07% and NNPA to 0.80% in FY24, supported by stringent credit evaluation and robust risk management. [↗](https://aptusindia.s3.amazonaws.com/Aptus+Value+Housing+Finance+India+Ltd_Annual+Report+FY24.pdf)
- Small business loans support local entrepreneurship and constitute 20% of the consolidated Assets Under Management (AUM). [↗](https://aptusindia.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/Aptus_AR_Mar+2025.pdf)
- Home loans are expected to drive growth due to government interest subsidy schemes and urbanisation in smaller towns, with target growth of ~24%. [↗](https://aptusindia.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/Aptus_AR_Mar+2025.pdf)
- The LAP segment is facing moderated expansion due to regulatory constraints and compliance with principal business criteria (PBC). [↗](https://aptusindia.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/Aptus_AR_Mar+2025.pdf)

### Home First Finance
- Assets Under Management (AUM) reached ₹15,878 Crore, a 24.9% YoY increase, driven by record quarterly disbursements of ₹1,572 Crore. [↗](https://homefirstindia.com/files/HomeFirst_Q4FY26_Press_Release.pdf)
- Network expansion added 16 branches and 12 touchpoints in FY26, bringing the total footprint to 171 branches and 373 touchpoints. [↗](https://homefirstindia.com/files/HomeFirst_Q4FY26_Press_Release.pdf)
- Strategic deployment of an in-house AI platform supporting seven Indian languages and Spark-powered ETL pipelines for advanced risk modeling. [↗](https://homefirstindia.com/files/Investor%20Deck%20Q4FY26.pdf)
- Housing loans remain the core product segment, constituting 83% of the total Assets Under Management (AUM). [↗](https://homefirstindia.com/files/Investor%20Deck%20Q4FY26.pdf)
- LAP constitutes 16% of the portfolio, targeting credit-tested customers within the affordable housing market. [↗](https://homefirstindia.com/files/Investor%20Deck%20Q4FY26.pdf)
- Management maintains a credit cost guidance range of 30 to 40 basis points as the company continues to scale. [↗](https://homefirstindia.com/files/HomeFirst_Q4FY26_Press_Release.pdf)

### India Shelter Finance
- Gross Asset Under Management (AUM) reached Rs. 11,044 crores in Q4FY26, representing a 27% year-on-year growth. [↗](https://india-shelter.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/uploads/1777716210890-Investor%20Presentation%20intimation%20%283%29.pdf)
- The company expanded its distribution moat to a Pan-India network of 307 branches across 15 states. [↗](https://india-shelter.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/uploads/1777716210890-Investor%20Presentation%20intimation%20%283%29.pdf)
- Significant digital adoption with Account Aggregator penetration reaching 94% on the sanctioned loan base by Q4FY26. [↗](https://india-shelter.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/uploads/1777716210890-Investor%20Presentation%20intimation%20%283%29.pdf)
- Profitability remains robust with PAT reaching Rs. 138 crores in Q4FY26, an 11% growth quarter-on-quarter. [↗](https://india-shelter.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/uploads/1777716210890-Investor%20Presentation%20intimation%20%283%29.pdf)
- Home loans constitute 67% of the total loan book, focusing on low and middle-income segments building or buying their first homes. [↗](https://india-shelter.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/uploads/1777716210890-Investor%20Presentation%20intimation%20%283%29.pdf)
- Self-employed borrowers represent 76% of the company's total customer base as of Q4FY26. [↗](https://india-shelter.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/uploads/1777716210890-Investor%20Presentation%20intimation%20%283%29.pdf)

### Aavas Financiers
- Gross Non-Performing Assets (GNPA) have shown a rising trend, increasing from 1.08% to 1.24% within the reporting periods of 2025. [↗](https://www.aavas.in/uploads/pdf/outcome-46562990.pdf)
- Net Non-Performing Assets (NNPA) have escalated from 0.73% to 0.85% by September 2025, reflecting a deterioration in the loan portfolio. [↗](https://www.aavas.in/uploads/pdf/outcome-46562990.pdf)
- The Provision Coverage Ratio (PCR) has declined from 60.87% to 58.27%, suggesting a relative reduction in the buffer against bad loans. [↗](https://www.aavas.in/uploads/pdf/outcome-46562990.pdf)
- A significant volume of loans assigned through co-lending and transfers remain unrated, potentially increasing credit risk visibility concerns. [↗](https://www.aavas.in/uploads/pdf/outcome-46562990.pdf)
- Net Profit Margin has faced compression, dropping from 24.11% in earlier disclosures to 22.17% by mid-2025. [↗](https://www.aavas.in/uploads/pdf/financials-85794283.pdf)

### Aptus Value Housing Finance
- The Loan Against Property (LAP) segment is facing moderated expansion due to regulatory constraints, including Principal Business Criteria (PBC) and monthly sell-down reporting norms. [↗](https://aptusindia.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/Aptus_AR_Mar+2025.pdf)
- Borrowers in the affordable housing segment often lack formal income documentation, making them more vulnerable to delinquency during economic shocks like inflation spikes. [↗](https://aptusindia.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/Aptus_AR_Mar+2025.pdf)
- Interest rate fluctuations can affect borrowing costs and customer affordability, potentially dampening housing demand or compressing margins for HFCs. [↗](https://aptusindia.s3.amazonaws.com/Aptus+Value+Housing+Finance+India+Ltd_Annual+Report+FY24.pdf)
- Asset quality showed slight deterioration in FY25, with Gross NPA increasing by 12 basis points to 1.19% and Net NPA rising by 9 basis points to 0.89%. [↗](https://aptusindia.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/Aptus_AR_Mar+2025.pdf)
- AUM growth is expected to slow to 22-23% in the current and subsequent fiscal years, compared to ~31% growth recorded in the previous fiscal. [↗](https://aptusindia.s3.us-east-1.amazonaws.com/Aptus_AR_Mar+2025.pdf)

### Home First Finance
- The company faces limited brand recognition in the affordable housing finance market when compared to established commercial banks. [↗](https://homefirstindia.com/files/Integrated%20Annual%20Report%20FY25.pdf)
- Home First's business model is sensitive to interest rate fluctuations and volatile market conditions which can impact margins. [↗](https://homefirstindia.com/files/Integrated%20Annual%20Report%20FY25.pdf)
- The company faces periodic competitive intensity within the housing finance industry. [↗](https://homefirstindia.com/files/Integrated%20Annual%20Report%20FY25.pdf)
- A high concentration (~70%) of the customer base belongs to the EWS/LIG category, making the portfolio sensitive to economic downturns. [↗](https://homefirstindia.com/files/HomeFirst_Q4FY26_Press_Release.pdf)
- Portfolio quality remains vulnerable to systemic shocks such as natural disasters or general economic downturns. [↗](https://homefirstindia.com/files/Integrated%20Annual%20Report%20FY25.pdf)

### India Shelter Finance
- The company faces risks from fluctuations in earnings and the ability to effectively manage rapid growth across its expanding branch network. [↗](https://india-shelter.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/uploads/1777716210890-Investor%20Presentation%20intimation%20%283%29.pdf)
- Exposure to restructured personal and housing loans remains a risk, with over 1,000 lakhs in accounts still classified under resolution plans. [↗](https://india-shelter.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/uploads/1777707511562-Financial+Results+March+2026.pdf)
- The company maintains a Gross NPA ratio of 1.25%, with a relatively low Provision Coverage Ratio on Stage 3 assets of 25.41%. [↗](https://india-shelter.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/uploads/1777707511562-Financial+Results+March+2026.pdf)
- Restructured loan accounts showed slippage during the period, with approximately 26.28 lakhs migrating to NPA status. [↗](https://india-shelter.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/uploads/1777707511562-Financial+Results+March+2026.pdf)
- The business is sensitive to prevailing economic interest rates and fiscal costs which can impact borrowing and lending margins. [↗](https://india-shelter.s3.ap-south-1.amazonaws.com/uploads/1777716210890-Investor%20Presentation%20intimation%20%283%29.pdf)
