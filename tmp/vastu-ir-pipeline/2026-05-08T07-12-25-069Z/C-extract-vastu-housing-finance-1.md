Management Discussion and Analysis
**Yield** Average portfolio yield remained broadly stable at
14.8% as of March 2025, compared to 15.0% in the previous
year—reflecting disciplined pricing amid a higher share of
housing loans.
**Average Ticket Size:** The portfolio remained 100% retail
and granular, with an average loan size of ₹13 lakh as of
March 2025, compared to ₹15 lakh in the previous year—
indicating deeper penetration in the mass-affluent and
emerging segments.

**Core Income:** Rose 23% YoY to ₹381 crore in FY25 from
₹310 crore, reflecting strong operating momentum.
**Operating Expenses:** Total opex increased 30% YoY
to ₹333 crore, with opex-to-ATA improving by 15 bps
YoY to 3.6% (adjusted for ₹24 crore ESOP cost and
strategic investments).
**Asset Quality**
Vastu continues to maintain prudent provisioning and
asset quality:
**GNPA/NNPA:** Gross Stage 3 assets rose slightly to 1.31%
of AUM (vs 0.89% in FY24), while Net Stage 3 assets stood
at 1.06% (vs 0.70%).
**Provisioning:** Credit cost stood at 0.43%, compared to
0.37% in FY24, supported by early resolution and strong
recovery performance.
**Coverage:** Provision Coverage Ratio on Stage 3 assets
was at 25%, while total ECL provisioning stood at 0.83%
of the book, up from 0.73% in FY24, demonstrating
conservative buffers.

**FY25 Key Financial and Operational**
**Highlights**
**Assets Under Management (AUM)**
AUM grew 42% YoY to ₹2,435 crore in FY25 from
₹1,717 crore in FY24, reflecting strong demand and deeper
market penetration.
**Disbursements**
Disbursements totalled ₹1,583 crore in FY25, growing 12%
YoY, despite macroeconomic headwinds in select markets.
**Net Interest Income (NII)**
NII increased 60% YoY to ₹157 crore in FY25 (from ₹98 crore
in FY24), in line with loan book expansion and improved
interest spreads.
**Securitisation**
Total securitisation reduced to ₹188 crore in FY25 (from ₹355
crore in FY24), resulting in a net loss of ₹9 crore from Direct
Assignment transactions, compared to ₹19 crore net income
in FY24.
