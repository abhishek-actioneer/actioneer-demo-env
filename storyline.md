# Sentinel Decks — Demo Fair Storyline
*1–2 minute teaser · Mixed audience (investors + operators) · Story-first · Anchors a live demo*

---

## Format

This is **not** a slide-by-slide presentation. It's a series of punchy anchor slides — big text, minimal words — that frame the live demo. Each slide lands a single idea. The presenter carries the narrative.

**5 slides total:**
1. What we believed
2. The pivot
3. The pain (with customer anchor)
4. The demo teaser → live demo
5. The vision

---

## Slide 1 — What We Believed
**Big headline:** "We thought people wanted Agents doing the work."
**Sub (small):** Autonomous. Background. Zero human in the loop.

*Narration:*
> "When we started Sentinel, we were convinced the future was full automation. Agents running in the background, doing the analysis, shipping the output. No human required."

---

## Slide 2 — The Pivot
**Big headline:** "The biggest decisions still happen in a room. Around a deck."

*Narration:*
> "But we got something wrong. Every major call — budgets, strategy, go/no-go — still happens with a team, a projector, and a slide deck."

---

## Slide 3 — The Pain
**Anchor number (huge):** `2–3 days`
**Sub:** just to prep for the review

**Then three quotes, stacked:**
> *"I don't have that number — let me come back to you."*
> *"We need to instrument that first."*
> *"Let's revisit this next week."*

**Small footnote:** A 100M+ user Vietnamese gaming publisher. 50-person data team. This is still their reality.

*Narration:*
> "A 50-person data team at one of Southeast Asia's largest gaming publishers spends 2 to 3 days every review cycle just preparing the deck. And even then — the meeting still ends with open questions."

---

## Slide 4 — The Demo Teaser
**Big headline:** "What if the deck prepared itself?"
**Sub:** Upload it to Sentinel. →

*Then: go live.*

*Narration:*
> "We took a different angle. Instead of replacing the ritual — we supercharged it. Here's how."

*(Switch to live demo of the Decks feature.)*

---

## Slide 5 — The Vision
**Big headline:** "Decision infrastructure."
**Sub (small, muted):** Not a dashboard. Not a chatbot. The layer between your data and your decisions.

*Narration:*
> "That's what we're building. The infrastructure that makes the people in the room sharper, every time."

---

## Key Principles
- Every slide = one idea. No bullets, no lists.
- Numbers are the only decoration. Make them huge.
- The live demo is slide 4's payload — the teaser slide just opens the door.
- "Agents" throughout — never "AI".
