## Key Definitions (from HDFC AMC vocabulary)
- **AUM** — Assets Under Management as on end of any given month/period
- **MAAUM** — Monthly Average AUM (a given month's average)
- **QAAUM** — Quarterly Average AUM (a given quarter's average)
- **AAUM** — Annual Average AUM (a given year's average)
- **T-30** — Top 30 cities by mutual fund AUM
- **B-30** — Beyond top 30 cities
- **Live Accounts** — Folios with non-zero balance
- **Unique Investors** — Identified by PAN or PEKRN (includes all unit holders & guardians)
- **Equity-oriented** — Includes equity-oriented hybrid funds; excludes arbitrage
- **Debt** — Includes debt-oriented index funds
- **Others** — Includes ETF, arbitrage, FOF-investing overseas
- **Liquid** — Money market is classified as debt (not liquid)
- **Operating Margin** — Revenue from operations minus total expenses (as bps of AAUM)
- **Systematic Transactions** — Includes SIP (Systematic Investment Plan) and STP (Systematic Transfer Plan)
