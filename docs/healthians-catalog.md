## Notes

- **Pricing is nationally uniform** — confirmed live scrape (May 2026) across metros and tier2 cities. Same price in all 215 cities. No city-tier differential. Exception: Pediatric Blood Culture varies by city.
- **~70% discount on MRP** is permanent everywhere. MRP is the inflated rack rate; Price is what customers pay.
- **City availability varies** — not all packages available in all cities. Category pages show tests regardless; the booking URL confirms availability. Basic tests (CBC, thyroid, glucose) available everywhere. Specialised packages have limited city coverage.
- **Family bundle pricing** — booking same package for 2 people in one visit: ~50% per-person discount. ~9% of bookings use this structure.
- **Parameters / Params:** number of individual measurements in the panel (e.g. CBC = 25 individual blood values). A package of 65 "tests" contains multiple organ panels grouped together.
- **Home collection:** free phlebotomist home visit + digital report in 6–27 hrs included on all SKUs.
- **Pediatric minimum age:** 5 years for most tests. Pediatric Blood Culture covers 0–15 years.
- **Source:** scraped live from healthians.com — 30+ category pages (risk/, habit/, popular-package, package detail pages).
- **Not captured:** individual allergy panel items (200+ allergens), radiology/scan tests, Govt. panel tests, ECG/EEG/PFT — estimated 500–1,000+ additional tests in backend catalog.
