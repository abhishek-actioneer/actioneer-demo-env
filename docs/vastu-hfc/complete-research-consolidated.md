- FY23: ₹80 Cr | FY24: ₹116 Cr | FY25: ₹36 Cr | 9M FY26: ₹80 Cr
- Securitisation/assignment gains are lumpy — they distort PAT quarter-to-quarter
- FY25 PAT was flat vs FY24 despite 25% interest growth because dereco gains collapsed

- CRAR at 68% — massively over-capitalized

- This is the growth runway — more borrowing = more lending = more interest income

- This was likely the Naspers/TA Associates/IFC funding round
