## Distilled Additions

The EFL deck suggests these additions to `tufte-analytical-slides.md`:

- Deck-level orientation maps and repeated agenda states.
- Summary-to-deep-dive cadence for long analytical chapters.
- One argument may require multiple tightly coupled proof objects.
- Case vignettes need context, intervention, impact, and transfer logic.
- Qualitative evidence should be treated as data when it explains operating behavior.
- Multi-criteria decision displays should show criteria and recommendation in the same frame.
- Transformation-program evidence should expose cadence, ownership, tools, deliverables, and required client commitment.
